Document identities and dependency facts were retrieved from the official IETF
Datatracker API. Published draft bytes are excluded when a Datatracker
`became_rfc` relation binds the draft identity to one of the included RFCs, so
the capacity estimate cannot count both versions of the same publication.

The frozen raw files and Datatracker responses remain local measurement inputs.
The tracked config and matrix bind their hashes, token counts, exact oracle
spans, relation plan, and the unchanged near-duplicate threshold.
