# P63 public redistribution evidence check

Checked 2026-09-09 against current official pages. This is a source-policy
evidence check for the proposed public dataset, not a determination of every
possible permitted use or legal exception.

- The [AMD investor-relations homepage](https://ir.amd.com/) links its footer's
  Terms and Conditions to AMD's main legal terms.
- [AMD Terms of Use / Copyright](https://www.amd.com/en/legal/copyright.html)
  describes personal/informational access and restricts reproducing, distributing
  or modifying materials for public/business purposes; it states that additional
  sub-site conditions or separately granted rights can apply. This is not an
  explicit open-data redistribution grant for the four P63 filings.
- [Amazon's investor document page](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/contact-us-and-request-documents/)
  provides downloads and printed-report requests. That inspected page establishes
  availability, not an explicit dataset redistribution license.
- The [Alphabet annual report](https://abc.xyz/assets/investor/static/pdf/2022_alphabet_annual_report.pdf)
  states that filings are accessible free of charge. This does not itself supply
  an explicit redistribution permission for this proposed full-text corpus.

Consequently, the current technical taskbank cannot truthfully be marked as an
openly licensed, public-release corpus. Retain the source links and existing
local provenance. Do not replace missing rights with a local HMAC signature or
infer that SEC filing availability makes third-party authored reports public
domain. The other issuers' terms and any separately held permissions were not
certified in this bounded check.

The local preparation of a validated training input and its engineering tests
are separate from claiming public distribution rights or measured training
benefit. No permission request was sent to a third party, and no public upload
was performed.
