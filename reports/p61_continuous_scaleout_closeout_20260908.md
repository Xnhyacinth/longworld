## Filtering is now enforced in the active pipeline

The known singleton-codebook check runs before cache reuse and before MiniLM
ranking. Existing green receipts cannot bypass it. The active pipeline also
requires readable primary candidates, valid question/identity/band fields,
complete primary-band coverage and matching candidate digests in each reused
audit. The resume fingerprint binds parent/candidate/audit bytes, primary scope,
and filter-rule identity. It does not substitute for source-signature validation
or a complete semantic/model evaluation.

Malformed inputs fail their own job and invalidate an old green filter state.
Filter outputs reject symlinks/non-regular destinations and are atomically
replaced; a failed receipt write is reported without aborting unrelated jobs.
All reproduced regressions were tested before fixing them. Independent temporary
directory checks confirmed that audit bytes survive symlink/directory attacks.

The sole watch is **PID 2595444**, with **8 task workers / up to 3 audit workers
per task**, a 900-second interval and the same exclusive pipeline lock. It was
restarted only after verifying the prior watcher had no child tasks. Its first
17-job tick classified nine jobs and blocked eight: seven known codebook tasks
and one existing artifact-count limit. AMD subsequently extends the catalog to
18 completed/blocked jobs. There is no claim of a perpetually saturated queue.
The source-expansion and native CodeForge batch processes have completed.

Prior filters were archived, and root verified all affected candidate/audit bytes
unchanged. No CURRENT_RELEASE or HF update, GPU training or change to unrelated
old SSH processes was made.

## Reading holds extend beyond the original IETF diagnostic

The final read-only audit covers all 30 products / 264 rows:

- Known question-only codebook answers: **18 rows**.
- Requested, undisclosed patch hashes: **24 rows**.
- Requested, undisclosed ancestry source IDs: **33 rows**.

These flags overlap. They identify **51 direct counterexamples**, then expand by
the same world/program to **66 task-scope hold rows: 57 train and 9 eval**. This
prevents an unflagged CF/length sibling being treated as cleared automatically.
Neither the remaining 213 direct non-hits nor the 198 rows outside task holds is
a certification of general reading safety. No new model-accuracy evaluation or
exhaustive strong-retriever test was performed.

The new reading v2 does not ask for unavailable digests or ancestry IDs. It returns
visible tag/files/review/test facts; hidden ancestry is a source-admission check,
not something the model is asked to infer. Its counterfactual changes an actual
visible CI result and changes the answer. Every returned field was checked in
the packed context, and source/patch-removal checks pass. The old hash/recovery
products and the unqualified paths-only v1 are preserved rather than relabeled.

New train rows had no detected repo/CIK/workflow-identifier intersection with the
existing eval set. This is an identifier-level check, not full content-leakage
or cross-product deduplication certification. The historical eval holds matter;
all 18 physical eval rows must not be assumed reading-ready.
