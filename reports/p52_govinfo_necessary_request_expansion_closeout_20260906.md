request needs its real source/target sections and the authenticated transition.

new oracle. Source artifact IDs, chronology, whole sections, filler ordering,

The freshly rebuilt 32K context had to equal the raw-checked prefix context
exactly. Full parent output was written only after the proof-growth prechecks

## Limits and downstream handoff

The current builder selects modified pairs only: factual answers are all **M**;
the one authentic D02 counterfactual changes that entry to **R**. More requested
keys improve actual dependence but do not balance labels, create a new task
family, or constitute independent worlds. This remains a source-text task,
not a claim about substantive legal effect.

The 32K raw checks are unsigned direct shared-function diagnostics. They do not
replace the complete task proof, 64K/128K raw checks, dense retrieval, source

`train_ready=false` and inventory delta zero.
