# P66 Macro scientific hold

The 324 BEA SFT views are excluded from the P66 training handoff after an
independent post-build audit. The native build and replay remain useful
infrastructure evidence, but they do not meet the requested long-dependency
admission standard.

All answer programs consume observation records, while the exact 64K/128K/256K
contexts obtain most of their length from derived relation records that the
programs never read. With relations removed, the corresponding observation-only
contexts contain 19,953–28,195, 42,063–56,106, and 89,458–106,246 tokens; none
reaches its labeled lower bound. The upstream source manifest also remains
diagnostic-only with generation integration and semantic train readiness false,
and the P66 config contains no replacement local-derivative authorization.

The questions additionally request canonical JSON without enumerating the
program-specific output keys. This makes exact-match evaluation under-specified.
The three length variants are correlated views of 108 semantic tasks over 36
series-period worlds and must not be counted as 324 independent tasks.

A valid rebuild may use observation-only contexts, which support natural 64K
and 128K capacity from 195,342–230,065 source tokens per complete series. It
must either omit 256K or add genuinely relevant official observations, specify
the output schema in each question, record a compatible local derivative
authorization, and rerun compact/window/remove-one controls. Current admission
is zero rows; strict, training-release and production eligibility remain false.
